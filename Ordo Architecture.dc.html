Ordo · System architecture · v1.0

Autonomous manufacturing orchestration

Agents run pipelines of production stages against live plant data. People set the autonomy level, approve gates and validate changes with evidence. Every decision and signature lands in an append-only audit ledger.

Key flows
1
SenseMachines publish signals through the edge gateway to the event bus; the plant state model updates in near real time.
2
DecideAn agent proposes an action with its reason and confidence. The policy guard allows, escalates or blocks it.
3
ActAllowed actions flow down as job orders and setpoints; the pipeline engine advances the stage on its success criteria.
4
Validate & signAt gates, the validation service bundles checks and evidence; people sign in sequence and the gate releases.
